N0IR: A BRIEF HISTORY

N0IR is an organization with an unusually long history. Its existence predates the digital age by several centuries.

Although N0IR has existed since 1444, the organization did not officially receive its current name until 1946.

By 1950, N0IR had become an important influence in the popularization of computers, helping bring computing technology into the public eye.

THE HISTORICAL TIMELINE

1444

THE FIRST APPEARANCE OF N0IR

N0IR canonically begins its existence. Its activities during this early period are associated with what would later be called the Duck Prevention Era.

ARCHIVE STATUS: HISTORICALLY SIGNIFICANT
1444-1949

THE DUCK PREVENTION ERA

The period before digital technology is known in N0IR's historical terminology as the Duck Prevention Era.

The name is officially recognized by N0IR historians. Its terminology is considered an important part of the organization's historical records.

PERIOD CLASSIFICATION: PRE-DIGITAL
1946

THE OFFICIAL NAMING OF N0IR

N0IR officially receives its name. The year is a deliberate reference to the creation of the term "film noir".

Despite receiving its name in 1946, the organization itself had already existed for centuries.

NAMING EVENT: CONFIRMED
1950

THE COMPUTER REVOLUTION

N0IR becomes an important organization due to its involvement in helping computers become popular.

This marks a major turning point in N0IR's history and its influence on the development of the digital world.

SIGNIFICANCE: MAJOR HISTORICAL MILESTONE

ARCHIVE FOOTNOTES

N0IR has existed since 1444, received its name in 1946, and became influential in 1950.

These are three separate historical milestones. They should not be confused with one another.
